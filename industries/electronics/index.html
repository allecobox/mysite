What Electronics Packaging Needs

Drop & crush protection

Correct flute selection (B, C or BC corrugated) and edge reinforcement sized to the weight of the device — not guesswork. We test stacking strength before bulk production.

Engineered inserts

EVA foam, molded pulp or corrugated inserts hold the device, cables and accessories in fixed positions so nothing shifts in transit.

Anti-scratch interior

Black kraft or white-lined board keeps the inside surface clean; gloss lamination outside resists scuffs on retail shelves.

E-commerce ready

Mailer boxes with tear strips and double adhesive tape survive parcel networks and make returns easy — the standard for D2C electronics brands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which box protects electronics best in shipping?

A double-wall corrugated transport box with an engineered insert (EVA foam or molded corrugated) for the specific device. The insert does most of the protective work; the box provides stacking strength and outer print. Both start at MOQ 100 pcs.

Can you produce anti-static or ESD packaging?

Yes, on request. Anti-static corrugated and conductive foam inserts are available for PCB-level and sensitive electronic components. Mention ESD requirements when requesting your quote.

Do you support private-label electronics brands?

Yes. Most of our electronics work is OEM/private-label: your logo, your artwork, your insert layout, produced in our own factory under NDA if required.

How do you make sure the box fits my device exactly?

Send the product dimensions or a physical sample. We produce a 3–5 day sample with the insert for your approval before bulk production — the dieline and structural design are free.
