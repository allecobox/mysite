What Food Packaging Needs

Food-contact safety

We use food-grade white board and grease-resistant coatings suitable for direct food contact, with food-safe soy-based inks on request.

Show the product

Window boxes with a transparent PET film let customers see the actual product — the single strongest conversion driver for bakery and confectionery retail.

Grease & moisture resistance

Fried, frozen and moist foods need PE or aqueous coatings. We match the coating to the food type so the box holds up through delivery.

Flat-pack efficiency

Fold boxes ship flat and assemble in one motion — critical for restaurants and cloud kitchens where storage space and assembly speed cost money.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your food boxes food-safe?

Yes. We use food-grade white board and coatings suitable for direct food contact, and food-safe soy-based inks are available on request. Tell us the food type — fried, frozen, oily or dry — and we specify the correct board and coating for it.

Can food boxes have a window to show the product?

Yes. Box with Window styles use a transparent PET or rPET film die-cut to any shape. Window boxes start at MOQ 500 pcs with prices from $0.25 per unit.

Do you produce for wholesale quantities?

Yes. We are a factory, not a trader — most food packaging projects run from 1,000 to 100,000+ units. MOQ starts at 100 pcs for standard structures, and unit price drops 20–40% at the 300–500 pcs tiers.

Can you print my logo on takeaway boxes in small quantities?

Yes. Food boxes and fold boxes start at MOQ 100 pcs, which is low enough for a single restaurant location to brand all of its takeaway packaging.
