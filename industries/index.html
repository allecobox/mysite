What Changes From One Industry to the Next

The box structure — how it folds and closes — is shared across industries. What changes is everything around it: board grade, coating, insert type, panel space for regulated copy and the finish that reads as premium to your customer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you make packaging for industries not listed here?

Yes. These four industries are our most common orders, but we produce custom boxes for apparel, candles, supplements, pet products and many other categories. The fastest way to check is to send your product details on WhatsApp — we reply with the right structure within 24 hours.

Can I order different box types for one product line?

Yes, and most brands do — for example a rigid gift box for the retail shelf and a mailer box for e-commerce fulfilment. Ordering multiple styles in one production run often reduces tooling cost.
