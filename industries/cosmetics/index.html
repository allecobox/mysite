What Cosmetics Packaging Needs

Premium shelf presence

Soft-touch or matte lamination, hot foil stamping in gold or rose gold, and spot UV on the logo are the finishes cosmetic brands order most.

Fitted inserts

Dropper bottles, jars and palettes must not move in transit. We die-cut cardboard or mould EVA inserts to hold each component in place.

Label & compliance space

Ingredient lists and net quantity declarations need planned panel space — we structure the dieline around your required copy.

Unboxing experience

Magnetic closures, ribbon pulls and printed inner liners turn a routine order into a shareable unboxing moment for social media.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best box style for a skincare set?

A rigid magnetic gift box with a fitted insert is the most popular choice for 3–5 piece skincare sets. It protects the glass components, looks premium on the shelf and supports foil and soft-touch finishes. MOQ is 500 pcs with prices from $0.50 per unit.

Can you match an exact Pantone colour for my brand?

Yes. Full CMYK printing is included in every quote, and we support Pantone spot colours for exact brand matching. Send your Pantone code with the artwork and we print a colour proof for approval before bulk production.

Do you offer sustainable materials for cosmetics?

Yes. We offer FSC-certified boards, recycled greyboard and soy-based inks. Many cosmetic brands pair a kraft or recycled rigid box with a premium finish like embossing to keep the look sustainable and high-end at the same time.

What is the MOQ for custom cosmetic boxes?

It depends on the structure: folding cartons and insert boxes start at 100 pcs, tray and lid boxes at 300 pcs, and magnetic rigid gift boxes at 500 pcs. See the full price list on our Custom Box Cost page.
