What Jewelry Packaging Needs

Compact rigid structures

Small boxes demand precision: tight tolerances, clean edges and dense greyboard so a 60×60 mm box still feels substantial in the hand.

Inserts that hold the piece

Rings, necklaces and earrings must sit exactly where the customer expects them. We die-cut velvet, foam or flocked inserts to your SKU dimensions.

Premium finishes

Hot foil in gold, silver or rose gold, debossed logos, soft-touch lamination and matte spot UV define the luxury look at this price point.

The unboxing moment

Drawer trays, two-piece lift-off lids and ribbon pulls slow the reveal down — the reason jewelry has the highest unboxing-video rate of any category.

Frequently Asked Questions

What box style do jewelry brands order most?

Two: the compact rigid gift box with a velvet or foam insert for individual pieces, and the drawer-style tray box for a premium reveal. Gift boxes start at $0.50 per unit at MOQ 500 pcs; tray boxes at $0.25 at MOQ 300 pcs.

Can you make inserts for specific product dimensions?

Yes — that is standard for us. Send the exact dimensions of each piece (or a sample) and we die-cut the insert to hold it precisely. Velvet, flocked, EVA foam and molded cardboard inserts are all available.

Is my jewelry design kept confidential?

Yes. We sign NDAs on request, production is in-house in our own factory (no subcontracting), and we never display client artwork without written permission.

What finishes work best for luxury jewelry boxes?

Hot foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold) on a soft-touch matte lamination is the most requested combination. Debossing and spot UV add depth for a higher-end feel at a modest setup cost.
