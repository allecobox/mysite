What Drives the Cost of a Custom Box?

1. Size & board grade

Bigger boxes use more material. Heavier greyboard or corrugated flute raises cost per square metre.

2. Printing

Full CMYK is standard and included. Pantone spot colours add a plate cost — worth it for exact brand colours.

3. Surface finishing

Matte/gloss lamination is low-cost. Foil stamping, spot UV and embossing each add a setup fee.

4. Inserts & structure

Custom foam, EVA or cardboard inserts add cost but protect high-value products. Standard structures cost less than fully custom dies.

5. Order quantity

The biggest lever. Moving from 100 to 300–500 pcs typically cuts unit price by 20–40% because setup cost is shared.

4 Ways to Lower Your Unit Price

Order the next MOQ tier

If cash flow allows, 300–500 pcs almost always beats 100 pcs on unit economics.

Simplify finishing on small runs

Skip foil and embossing below 500 pcs — a strong print + matte lamination still looks premium.

Trim the box size

A 10% smaller footprint saves material on every unit and lowers shipping cost too.

Ask for a cost-optimised alternative

Send your design — our engineers often find a structure that looks the same but costs less to make.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom box cost?

Most custom boxes cost $0.25–$1.99 per unit at MOQ. The lowest is a simple square box from $0.08/pc at 100 pcs; complex handled or windowed gift boxes reach $2.99/pc. Your exact price depends on size, board grade, printing and finishing.

Why do custom box prices vary so much?

Five factors drive price: box size, board grade and thickness, number of print colours (CMYK vs Pantone), surface finishing (lamination, foil, spot UV, embossing), and order quantity. A larger order spreads setup cost and lowers the unit price.

How can I lower the unit cost of my custom box?

Increase order quantity to the next MOQ tier, simplify finishing on low-quantity runs, reduce box size where possible, and choose a standard structure instead of a fully custom die. Send your design and our engineers will suggest a cost-optimised alternative.
