What to include in your inquiry

The more of these five you send, the closer the first quote will be to the final price. If you only have one, send that — we will ask for the rest.

  • 1Size — internal dimensions in mm or cm (L × W × H). If you only know the product size, we will calculate the box for you.
  • 2Quantity — even a rough range helps. 100 pcs and 5,000 pcs are priced on different equipment.
  • 3Box type — a name from our 15 structures, or just a photo of something similar.
  • 4Printing and finishing — full-colour artwork, logo only, foil, spot UV, embossing, matte or gloss lamination.
  • 5Target date and destination — when you need it in hand and which country or port it ships to, so we can quote DDP where available.
