Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which box type is right for my product?

Start with how the box will be used: shipping needs a Mail or Transport Box, retail needs a Product or Fold Box, and premium gifting needs a rigid Gift Box or Box with Lid. If your product is fragile or has multiple parts, add custom inserts. The finder above narrows all 15 types in under a minute.

Can I get a box type that isn't on this list?

Yes. The 15 structures cover most projects, but we also make fully custom dies for unusual shapes. Send a sketch or reference photo on WhatsApp and our structural engineers will propose the closest standard structure or a custom one, with a free dieline.

What if my quantity is below the MOQ for the box I want?

Six of our structures start at just 100 pcs, and we can sometimes flex on the rest for first-time customers. See our full MOQ guide for how minimums work and five ways to order below MOQ.

How much does each box type cost?

From $0.08 to $2.99 per unit depending on type, size, material and quantity. Our factory price list shows the real range for all 15 types, plus how prices drop at 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 pcs.
