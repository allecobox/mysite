Custom Box MOQ Guide: How to Order From Just 100 Pcs

"MOQ 5,000 — call us back when you're bigger." If you've heard that from packaging suppliers, this guide is for you. Here's why box factories set minimums, our real MOQs for all 15 box types (starting at 100 pcs), and 5 practical ways to get custom packaging even when you can't hit the minimum.

Quick answer: at AllecoBox, MOQ starts at 100 pcs for 9 of our 15 structures — including square, mail, transport, product, food, fold, hang and insert boxes. Display, tray and lid boxes start at 300 pcs; rigid gift boxes and window boxes at 500 pcs.

What Is MOQ, Exactly?

MOQ stands for Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest number of units a factory will produce in one run. It's not an arbitrary upsell. Every custom box order carries fixed setup costs that exist whether you print 50 boxes or 50,000:

  • Printing plates — one set per artwork, per colour
  • Die-cutting tooling — a custom steel die for your box shape
  • Machine make-ready — setup sheets and calibration wasted on every run start
  • Quality checks and packing — largely per-batch, not per-box

On a 100-pc run, those fixed costs might represent a third of your total price. On a 5,000-pc run, they're a rounding error. That's why unit prices drop so steeply with quantity — and why a factory that accepts 50 pcs either charges you triple per box or loses money.

Can't Hit the MOQ? 5 Things That Actually Work

  1. Drop to a 100-pc structure. Nine of our 15 types start at 100. A Fold Box with your artwork often achieves the same look as a more complex structure at a lower minimum.
  2. Stock box + custom sleeve or label. A plain stock box wrapped in a fully printed custom sleeve gives you a branded look with a much smaller commitment — ideal for pre-launch and market testing.
  3. One artwork, multiple sizes. If the design allows, we can gang several box sizes of the same artwork on one printed sheet, so a single setup covers your whole range.
  4. Flexible lead time. If your order can wait to share a machine run with another job, we can sometimes accept below-MOQ quantities. Worth asking — the answer is often yes.
  5. Trial run at a premium. We can quote 50 pcs for sampling or investor demos at a higher unit price. You'll know the exact premium upfront — no surprises on the invoice.

What Happens to Price as Quantity Grows?

Because setup costs are fixed, unit prices fall steeply at first and then flatten. A typical mid-size mail box drops roughly 35–40% going from 100 to 500 pcs, and 55–60% at 2,000 pcs. See the full 100 / 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 pcs comparison table on our custom box cost page.

Is MOQ Per Design or Per Order?

Per design and size combination, because each artwork and dieline needs its own setup. Two practical notes:

  • Multiple colour variants of the same box: if the layout allows, variants can be ganged on one sheet and share the die — effectively one MOQ for several SKUs.
  • Reorders of an existing design: no new plates or dies are needed, so reorders can often go below the first-order MOQ. Ask us when you reorder.

MOQ FAQs

What is the minimum order quantity for custom boxes?

At AllecoBox the MOQ starts at 100 pcs for 9 of our 15 box structures, including square, mail, transport, product, food, fold, hang and insert boxes. Complex structures like gift boxes start at 500 pcs because rigid board requires more hand-work and setup per run.

Why do custom box manufacturers set MOQs?

Every order carries fixed setup costs that exist no matter how few boxes you print: printing plates, die-cutting tooling, machine make-ready time and quality checks. A very small order means those fixed costs are spread over few units, pushing the per-box price up sharply. MOQs keep unit pricing viable for both sides.

Can I order custom boxes below the MOQ?

Sometimes. Practical options include: choosing a structure with a 100-pc MOQ, using a stock-size box with custom printed labels or sleeves, ordering with flexible artwork (one design, several box sizes), accepting a longer production window so your order shares a machine run, or asking us to quote a trial run at a higher unit price.

How much cheaper are custom boxes at higher quantities?

Significantly. On a typical mid-size mail box, moving from 100 to 500 pcs cuts the unit price by roughly 35–40%, and 2,000 pcs cuts it by 55–60%. Setup costs are fixed, so every extra unit lowers the average price. See our price list page for a full 100 / 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 pcs comparison table.

Is the MOQ per design or per order?

Per design and size combination, because each artwork and dieline needs its own setup. If you want three colour variants of the same box, either combine the variants into one printed sheet (if the design allows) or ask us to quote each variant separately — mixed SKUs in one order can sometimes share the die.
