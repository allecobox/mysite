What we actually do

We make the box a product arrives in. That covers everything from a $0.08 folding carton for a lip balm to a $2.99 rigid gift box with magnetic closure and foam insert. Most of our customers are small and mid-sized brands launching a product, switching suppliers, or fixing a packaging problem that their last factory could not solve.

Because we own the equipment, a change to your dieline is a conversation with the production team — not a negotiation with a middleman. Structural changes, board grade swaps, spot UV instead of matte lamination: these are decided on the floor, usually within a day.

How an order runs

Step 1

Send your spec

Size, quantity, box style, artwork or a reference photo. A sketch on paper is enough to start.

Step 2

Dieline in 24 hours

We send a to-scale dieline plus a 3D mockup so you can see the box before paying for tooling.

Step 3

Sample in 3–5 days

A physical sample with your artwork, printed on the board grade you selected. Approve it or adjust it.

Step 4

Bulk in 12–15 days

Production starts after sample approval. Photo and video updates go out throughout the run.

What sets your unit price

Six variables move the price. Knowing which one you are actually paying for is usually worth more than getting a 5% discount.

VariableEffect on priceWhere you can save
QuantityLargest single factor — moving from 100 to 5,000 pcs can cut unit cost by 30–50%Order the full year in one run if storage allows
Board gradeKraft, corrugated, rigid greyboard and specialty paper differ substantially per sqmUse corrugated where the box only needs to protect
Printing coverageFull-bleed CMYK costs more than a one-colour logo on kraftPrint inside-out only where the customer sees it
StructureMagnetic closures, drawers and multi-piece lids add assembly timeChoose a one-piece fold where the unboxing feel allows
FinishingFoil stamping, spot UV, embossing and soft-touch lamination are separate passesLimit to one premium finish, not three
SizeDriven by board area — a 10 mm change on a large box can cross a pricing tierCheck whether the product fits a standard footprint
