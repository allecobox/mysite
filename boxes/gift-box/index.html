Gift boxes in detail: rigid build, magnets, inserts and MOQRead more

Nearly everything on this page is a rigid box: 2 mm greyboard wrapped by hand in 157 gsm art paper, which is why the minimum sits at 500 pcs rather than 100. The wrap is what you are paying for. A soft-touch laminated wrap makes a magnetic closure feel heavier than it is, and spot UV over a matte surface gives the logo a raised edge without adding a separate foil pass. Flip-top board game boxes are the exception here — they are printed on 2 mm board and left unwrapped so the inside stays clean for the playing pieces.

The closure decides how the box is used. Magnetic boxes open flat to 180° and present the product lying down, which photographs well but needs 8–10 mm of clearance for the lid to swing. Drawer sleeves pull out sideways and work better for perfume and jewellery, where the product is taller than it is wide. If the item moves inside, an EVA or paperboard insert is cheaper than extra tissue: a die-cut cavity holds the shape and lets the buyer lift the product with one finger notch. Tell us whether the box ships assembled or flat, because it changes the packing cost on both sides.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom gift boxes?

Our MOQ starts from 500 pcs per size and per design for custom gift boxes. Typical factory pricing for this box type runs from $0.50 to $1.95 per unit at MOQ, depending on size, board grade, printing and finishing. Larger runs receive our best factory-direct unit price. Tell us your dimensions and we will confirm the exact tier in your quote.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard gift box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can gift boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every gift box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.

Which gift box structures can AllecoBox manufacture?

We manufacture all 99 structures shown on this page in our own Shenzhen factory, including magnetic closure, drawer, lid-and-base, window, ribbon-tie and corrugated gift boxes.

Need gift boxes built to your product?

Send your size, quantity and artwork — we reply with a free dieline and a factory quote within 24 hours.
