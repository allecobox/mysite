Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom product boxes?

Our MOQ starts from 100 pcs per size and per design for custom product boxes. Typical factory pricing for this box type runs from $0.25 to $0.99 per unit at MOQ, depending on size, board grade, printing and finishing. Larger runs receive our best factory-direct unit price. Tell us your dimensions and we will confirm the exact tier in your quote.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard product box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can product boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every product box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.

Which product box structures can AllecoBox manufacture?

We manufacture all 20 structures shown on this page in our own Shenzhen factory, including straight tuck, reverse tuck, crash-lock bottom, sleeve and hang-tab product boxes.

Need product boxes built to your product?

Send your size, quantity and artwork — we reply with a free dieline and a factory quote within 24 hours.
