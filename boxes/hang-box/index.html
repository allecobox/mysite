Hang boxes in detail: euro tabs, peg load and rear-open buildsRead more

Anything on this page is designed to hang, and the hang feature is where the engineering sits. A euro slot tab is punched into an extended top panel and folds flat for shipping; a vertical strip hole runs down the back panel and takes a peg through the middle of the box. The 49 layouts cover both, plus rear-open versions where the box swings forward on the peg and the product faces the customer, and a reverse mortise lock that keeps the flap shut after repeated opening. Polygonal cone shapes appear here too, because they hang well and use very little shelf depth.

Peg load is the number to give us. A euro tab on 350 gsm board holds roughly 500 g without reinforcement; past that the slot elongates and the box drops. A vertical strip hole spreads the load over the full height of the box and takes 1.5–2 kg comfortably. If the product is heavy, we add a doubled tab or a die-cut reinforcement ring rather than thicker board, because thickness does not solve a stress concentration. Tell us the peg spacing too — a 50 mm pitch needs the tab centred, while a 25 mm pitch gives you room to offset it.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom hang boxes?

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard hang box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can hang boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every hang box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.
