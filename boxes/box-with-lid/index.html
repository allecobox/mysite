Custom Lidded Boxes — 50 Structures

Lidded boxes in detail: lid fit, telescoping depth and shoe box buildsRead more

The 50 layouts on this page all separate into two parts, and the fit between them is the whole product. A lid that slides on with 1 mm of clearance feels precise on the first open and jams by the tenth; 2 mm feels loose but keeps working. We cut to 1.5 mm as a default and adjust if the box is going to a humid market. Telescoping lids — where the lid drops over the base by 30–50 mm rather than sitting on top — hide the seam and add stack strength, which is why shoe boxes use them.

Corrugated lidded boxes for shipping follow a different rule: the lid depth equals the base depth minus the board thickness, so the two halves meet flush instead of crushing each other. Window cake boxes on this page combine a lid with a film panel and need the film set back from the lid edge or it peels when the lid is lifted. If the box is going to be opened repeatedly in a shop, say so — we will add a thumb notch or a ribbon tab, both of which cost less than a returned box. A lid printed on the inside is a cheap upgrade and usually the first thing a customer sees when the box opens.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom lidded boxes?

Our MOQ starts from 300 pcs per size and per design for custom lidded boxes. Typical factory pricing for this box type runs from $0.25 to $1.99 per unit at MOQ, depending on size, board grade, printing and finishing. Larger runs receive our best factory-direct unit price. Tell us your dimensions and we will confirm the exact tier in your quote.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard lidded box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can lidded boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every lidded box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.

Which lidded box structures can AllecoBox manufacture?

We manufacture all 50 structures shown on this page in our own Shenzhen factory, including lift-off lid, shoulder neck, shoe box and two-piece lidded boxes.
