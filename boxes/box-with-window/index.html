Window boxes in detail: film choice, window shape and die-cut clearanceRead more

A window does two jobs at once: it shows the product and it removes printable area, so the layout has to be designed around the cut rather than adjusted afterwards. The 74 structures on this page include tuck-top cookie boxes with a square window, mountain-shaped boxes with a shaped window, biscuit boxes with a flip window that opens like a door, and full-face doll boxes where the film covers almost the entire front. Each one needs a different film and a different amount of glue margin.

Film is usually 0.3–0.5 mm PET: clear, anti-scratch, and it will not cloud at room temperature. PVC is cheaper and softer but yellows under sunlight, so we avoid it for anything that sits in a shop window. The window cut needs at least 12 mm of board between the film edge and the box edge, otherwise the panel flexes and the film lifts at the corner. Flip windows add a hinge line, and that line has to fall on a crease, not on the film. Send the dieline with the window drawn as a separate layer and we will check the clearance before the die is made. We keep window dies on file, so a repeat order in the same shape skips the die cost entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom window boxes?

Our MOQ starts from 500 pcs per size and per design for custom window boxes. Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard window box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can window boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every window box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.
