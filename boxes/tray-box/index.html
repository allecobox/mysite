Tray boxes in detail: drawer tolerance, sleeves and two-piece setsRead more

A tray box is a shallow open shell, and most of the 66 layouts here pair it with something else — a sleeve, a lid, or a second tray to make a drawer. That pairing is where the fit is won or lost. A drawer sliding inside a sleeve needs 0.5–1 mm of clearance per side: tighter and it jams in humid weather, looser and it drops out when the box is tilted. We cut the sleeve and the drawer from the same board lot so the two parts expand and contract together, which matters more than most buyers expect.

Two-piece sets work well for jewellery, chocolate and small electronics because the inner tray can be compartmented without extra tooling. A paper food tray follows the same logic at a lower price point: one die, no glue, nested for shipping. If the drawer needs a pull, a ribbon is the cheapest option and a die-cut finger notch the most durable. Tell us the total weight the tray will carry, because a single-layer base on 350 gsm board will bow past about 1.5 kg even when the sides stay straight. Sleeves can be printed on the inside as well, which turns an inexpensive drawer set into a presentation piece.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom tray boxes?

Our MOQ starts from 300 pcs per size and per design for custom tray boxes. Typical factory pricing for this box type runs from $0.25 to $1.99 per unit at MOQ, depending on size, board grade, printing and finishing. Larger runs receive our best factory-direct unit price. Tell us your dimensions and we will confirm the exact tier in your quote.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard tray box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can tray boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every tray box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.
