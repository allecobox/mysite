Mailer boxes in detail: flip-top, front-open and shipping strengthRead more

A mailer box has to survive a courier and still open like a gift, which is why the 61 layouts here split into two very different builds. The flip-top mailer is a single glued blank: it ships flat, folds up in seconds, and the lid hinges on the back wall so the customer sees the whole product at once. The front-open version puts the hinge on the bottom and the opening on the face instead, so the box can sit on a shelf and be opened without lifting it. Both take 350 gsm C1S or E-flute, and both can carry a tear strip if the parcel has to be tamper-evident.

Sizing is where most mailer orders go wrong. The internal dimension needs 5 mm of clearance on each side for the product plus any wrap, and another 3 mm if the box has a printed interior. A box cut to the exact product size will bulge at the lid and the hinge will tear after a few openings. If the parcel weighs more than 5 kg, we move the build to B-flute and add a double-wall base panel rather than thickening the whole sheet. Board game boxes on this page follow the same rule at a different scale: 2 mm board, four-side wrap, and a lid deep enough to clear a stack of cards.
