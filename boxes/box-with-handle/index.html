Handle boxes in detail: rope, die-cut and load ratingRead more

A handle is a load path. Everything above the product has to travel through two attachment points, so the 100 layouts on this page are really a study in how those points are reinforced. Die-cut handles are punched straight through the board and cost nothing extra, but they only work up to about 2 kg before the paper starts to tear at the slot ends. Rope handles are threaded through a reinforced patch or a plastic eyelet and carry 5 kg comfortably; PP rope is stronger and cheaper than cotton but feels industrial, which matters for a gift box and does not for a cake box.

The arched and mountain-shaped builds on this page get their strength from the fold rather than the board, so the handle sits on a double-thickness section. Cake boxes with a handle use a flat base and a tall gable so the box can be carried one-handed without the cake sliding; tray boxes with a handle do the same job for multiple items. Tell us the packed weight and whether the box is carried by hand or hung on a hook, because a handle designed for carrying will fail quickly if it is hung from a retail peg.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom handle boxes?

Our MOQ starts from 300 pcs per size and per design for custom handle boxes. Typical factory pricing for this box type runs from $0.45 to $2.99 per unit at MOQ, depending on size, board grade, printing and finishing. Larger runs receive our best factory-direct unit price. Tell us your dimensions and we will confirm the exact tier in your quote.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard handle box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can handle boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every handle box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.

Which handle box structures can AllecoBox manufacture?

We manufacture all 100 structures shown on this page in our own Shenzhen factory, including rope handle, die-cut handle, plastic handle and suitcase-style boxes.
