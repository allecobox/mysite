Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom display boxes?

Our MOQ starts from 300 pcs per size and per design for custom display boxes. Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample before production. Standard display box samples are ready in 3–5 days, and we provide a free dieline and 3D mockup at the design stage so you can check the structure first.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production normally takes 12–15 days after sample approval, depending on structure, finishing and quantity. We send photo and video updates throughout production and ship DDP worldwide.

Can display boxes be made in custom sizes and printed with my logo?

Yes. Every display box is made to your product dimensions, not from a fixed size list. Choose board grade and thickness, CMYK or Pantone printing, foil stamping, embossing, matte or gloss lamination, and custom inserts.
