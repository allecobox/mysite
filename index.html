FAQ

Questions About Custom Boxes

Which box type should I choose for my product?
Start from the product and the journey. Soft or fragile items usually need an insert box or a box with lid; retail shelf products work well in a box with window or a hang box; e-commerce shipping uses a mail box or transport box; presentation and gifting call for a gift box or a square rigid box. Send us your product dimensions and we will recommend the right structure.
What is the MOQ for custom printed boxes?
Standard MOQ starts at 300 pcs per design for rigid, gift and lid-and-base boxes, and 500 pcs for folding cartons, mail boxes and transport boxes. Trial runs and sample orders are welcome — we quote based on your real quantity.
How fast can I get a quote and a dieline?
Every inquiry is answered within 12 working hours. Once we have dimensions, quantity and artwork direction, you receive factory-direct pricing plus a free dieline and 3D mockup within 24 hours.
How long is sampling, production and shipping?
Sampling takes 3–5 days and mass production 12–18 days after artwork approval. We ship worldwide from Shenzhen by express, air or sea, with door-to-door DDP service to the US, EU, UK, Australia and the Middle East.
Do you offer FSC-certified and plastic-free options?
Yes. FSC-certified paperboard, recycled greyboard, water-based inks and plastic-free molded-pulp or paper-honeycomb inserts are available, with certification documents supplied for compliance and ESG reporting.
Can you match my brand color and logo exactly?
Yes. We print to Pantone references, offer CMYK offset and digital short runs, and provide a printed color proof or physical sample for approval so color stays consistent across reorder runs.
